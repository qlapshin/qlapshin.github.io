Cleanlean is a dietary supplement that helps to improve the quality of sleep and general condition of the body. 

The supplement contains L-theanine, GABA and melatonin. L-theanine promotes relaxation and reduces stress levels. GABA helps reduce anxiety and improve sleep quality. Melatonin regulates the circadian rhythms of the body and is responsible for sleep quality.

I designed the logo, packaging and promotional posters.
