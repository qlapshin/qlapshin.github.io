Greencup sells vegetarian coffee. The task was to create a “green” logo, which have a vegetarian focus, but will not alienate other people. I have created the logo and several other mediums, such as a business card, a menu and a coffee packaging.

The logo includes 3 essential images: a leaf — shows the natural origin, coffee, and a house — shows the cosiness of this place.
